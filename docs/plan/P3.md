## Task 1: Scrum

A - Scrum is the right choice because it fits this project’s small development team to work on it in small steps. The project contains features with different priorities that is a perfect fit into scrum’s workflow, where the highest priority backlogs get attention first. Development and testing will be performed in incremental steps and across iterations.

B – Some features such as the Realtime updates might be too difficult to implement. If that turns out to be the case, we will settle for  second updates with streams to the clients. Week 7 and especially 13 deadlines still have to be met no matter what.
